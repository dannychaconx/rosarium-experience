# Product Vision

## Problem
Flowers are often transactional. The emotional or spiritual meaning is not extended beyond the moment of delivery.

## Opportunity
Attach a simple digital ritual (prayer) to a physical product (rose), creating a repeatable spiritual habit.

## Core Experience
Scan → Reflect → Pray → Return (daily habit loop)

## Why Now
- QR behavior is normalized
- Mobile-first usage
- Desire for simple guided experiences

## Long-Term Vision
A global network of physical objects (roses, gifts) triggering meaningful digital rituals.
